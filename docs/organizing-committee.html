Want to Join Us?

We would like to sincerely thank our volunteers and reviewers. Without their dedication and effort, it is impossible to manage this online conference with removing the barriers (e.g. visa barriers + travel hassles) and zero cost of travel and greenhouse gas emissions.

Please keep spreading the word in your research community and international conferences. And encourage your colleagues in other disciplines to enable carbon-free cost-free conferencing for other research communities. We’re happy to provide guidance!

Volunteer Opportunities

If you’re interested in volunteering for BTR, we welcome contributions from the transportation research community. Volunteer roles include:

  • Session moderators
  • Technical support
  • Paper review
  • Social media promotion
  • Website maintenance
  • Registration assistance

To volunteer, please contact us at bridgingtransport@gmail.com with your area of interest and availability.
